# 📂 Dynamic Programming

**Dynamic Programming (DP)** is a technique for solving complex problems by breaking them down into simpler subproblems and storing their solutions to avoid redundant calculations. It is widely used for optimisation and decision-making.

## 📌 **Common Problems**

1. **Knapsack Problem**
2. **Longest Common Subsequence (LCS)**
3. **Fibonacci Sequence**
4. **Coin Change Problem**
5. **Matrix Chain Multiplication**

## Approach

### **1️⃣ Top-Down (Memoisation)**

- Recursively solves subproblems.
- Caches intermediate results to prevent duplicate computations.

### **2️⃣ Bottom-Up (Tabulation)**

- Iteratively builds solutions from the simplest cases.
- Often more efficient in terms of space and time.
